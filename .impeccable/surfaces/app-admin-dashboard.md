## Scope

`/admin/dashboard` and `/admin/pronostico`, desktop and mobile. Preserve attendance and operations. The user pinned month/year history, one chain/location context, clearer charts, local drilldown, model explanation, correlations, and removal of daily staffing cards.

## Audience, job, proof, constraints

Owner or operations manager deciding what changed, which location explains it, what needs attention, and what demand comes next. Proof is Fudo history, coverage, held-out WAPE, measured climate sensitivity, and observed correlations. Missing data, estimated cost, and correlation must never read as fact.
